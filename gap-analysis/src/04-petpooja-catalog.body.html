4. Feature catalog (A–X)

A. POS / billing

  • [[CORE]] Bill customisation (logo, bill break-ups, edit customer details, dynamic payment QR); e-bill receipts and e-bill messaging; keyboard + touchscreen; "200+ hardware" — [[PO]] billing page.

B. Menu management

  • [[CORE]] Item categories, variations and add-ons "with separate pricing for each customisation"; item shortcodes; multiple menus incl. separate menu per dine-in area; pricing control over physical and online menus; edit item/time/price and switch ON/OFF from the POS to aggregators; open/close menu by stock availability — [[PO]] menu page.
  • [[CORE]] Combos, group offers, variations, add-ons — [[PO]] QSR page (not on the menu page itself).
  • [[UNK]] Packaging charges are not stated on the menu page (the online-orders page mentions modifying "packaging" across aggregators); outlet-specific menu variants are not stated; central menu and price management across outlets is stated on the head-office page (see Q).
  • [[PO]] Menu-sync blog: fields synced to aggregators = item names and photos, prices, item on/off, categories and sort order, veg/non-veg mark and calories, taxes and charges, timing (breakfast/lunch); "a separate price tier per channel"; most price/availability changes reach apps "within a few minutes"; new items and photos wait for the aggregator's review; on/off is fastest "in seconds".

C–E. Tables, kitchen, captain

  • [[ADD]] Scan & Order (QR) — unique QR per table generated from the Menu module; each table keeps its own bill across ordering rounds; customer receives an OTP and the captain validates it before the KOT fires; browser-based (no app); pay at counter or online (Scan & Pay is a separate add-on); sold-out toggle updates the QR menu instantly; requires internet on both the customer phone and the POS — [[PO]] scan-and-order.
  • [[ADD]] Kitchen Display System — shows online and dine-in orders; replaces paper KOT; individual KOT view and aggregated item view; mark food ready; kitchen marks items out of stock and captains/billers are notified; multiple cooking stations; Android and Windows, any Android phone. Routing rules, timers and price not stated — [[PO]] KDS.
  • [[ADD]] Captain Ordering App — Android phones/tablets; multiple orders and bill settlement; UPI/wallet, contactless card under ₹5,000, cash, QR; offline architecture with POS sync; AI recommendation engine for upselling; AI voice recognition for item entry; captains assigned to tables/areas from the POS. KOT, modifiers and table transfer/merge are not described on the page — [[PO]] captain app.
  • [[ADD]] Token Management — Android app on an Android TV (with Play Store) showing pickup orders, live status and token numbers; voice alert when food is marked ready; mark ready from POS or KDS; Zomato/Swiggy OTP check-in for delivery executives; works online and offline — [[PO]] token.
  • [[ADD]] Wireless Calling Device (hardware) — "LED colours kept on the table, allowing customers to call for the waiter" — [[PO]] marketplace.
  • [[ADD]] Table Reservation Manager (formerly Reservation Manager) — syncs Zomato Dining, District, EazyDiner; own branded booking link (optional refundable deposit; shareable via Instagram/WhatsApp/website); phone bookings and walk-ins in one inbox; queue management (guests order while waiting); auto table assignment by party size and live floor status; cancellation alerts; automated reminders; WhatsApp feedback requests after visits; syncs with POSS — [[PO]] reservation manager.

Offline and architecture (Phase 12 input)

[[PO]] POS Local: a Bridge Server runs on a Windows computer at the restaurant and holds a local database with every bill, order and report. Counters read/write it directly; one device is the Master Billing Station and secondary counters, kitchen displays and captain devices connect through it. If the Bridge Server and POS are on the same machine billing runs fully offline; if on separate machines it "continues for up to 5 days offline". Menu/price updates, settings and aggregator orders sync down from Petpooja's cloud when the connection returns; pending online orders "catch up on their own". Scheduled local backups (daily/weekly/monthly) go to a separate drive. The cloud POS variant also works through drops and syncs bills when connectivity returns.
