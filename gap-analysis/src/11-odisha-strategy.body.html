What could be verified is listed with sources; everything else is a hypothesis tagged [[INF]] and must be validated with customer interviews before it drives spending. No market-size figure is invented, no legal claim is made (check GST/FSSAI/licensing points with a CA or lawyer), and "#1" is treated as a set of measurable milestones, not an assumption.

25.1 Odisha restaurant market analysis

Verified or sourced facts

  • [[TP]] A press report on the launch of the NRAI Odisha chapter (Bhubaneswar, 5 Dec 2025) says the state has "roughly 8,000 food and beverage establishments"; the chapter targets 1,500 member businesses within two years; growth is noted in Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, Rourkela and Puri; GST rationalisation is named as an industry concern. The same report quotes the food-services sector at "13.09% of GDP" and a 2023–24 growth projection of 10.57% without a clear source — treat as unverified. Source: Prameya News. An association exists → a partnership channel (see 25.4).
  • [[TP]] Tourism-linked demand: press reports describe hotels in Puri near full occupancy in holiday periods and a 22% rise in Puri visitor arrivals in the first five months of 2026; a snippet cites 24.3 million tourists for Puri in 2025, but its own breakdown (11.3 million domestic + 87,000 foreign) does not sum to that figure → treat the number as unreliable. Sources: ETV Bharat, Odisha Tourism (Rath Yatra 27 Jun–5 Jul 2025).
  • [[TP]] Payments: UPI is reported at roughly 85% of India's digital payments in FY2025-26; monthly volume 23.2 billion transactions in May 2026; small merchants receiving under ₹1 lakh/month through UPI QR are reported exempt from new merchant-discount charges introduced in 2026, and a 15 Sep 2026 article says UPI "got a price tag" for select transactions. Sources: Meetanshi, Tech Portal. Verify current rules with NPCI/RBI before designing UPI pricing.

25.3 Odisha-first customer experience (onboarding friction)

[[V]] Today there is no onboarding wizard (Part 06). Recommended features that reduce time-to-first-bill, ranked by friction removed [[REC]]:

  1. Menu import from a photo/Excel/PDF of the menu (Excel exists; add CSV from other POS; the migration-transformer plan on the earlier page applies) — the largest single onboarding cost.
  2. GST setup wizard (GSTIN, tax rates, inclusive/exclusive, service charge yes/no) with safe defaults reviewed by a CA-authored template.
  3. Printer setup guided by tested model list and one-click test print (needs the print agent).
  4. Tables/areas quick-create ("20 tables, 2 floors").
  5. UPI QR setup by entering a VPA.
  6. Staff creation from role templates (owner, cashier, captain, kitchen).
  7. WhatsApp connect via Embedded Signup — hide until after first bill.
  8. Local-language help: short Odia and English video tutorials embedded on each setup step; WhatsApp support button; assisted remote onboarding session for the first restaurants; on-site visits in Bhubaneswar/Cuttack for premium plans.
