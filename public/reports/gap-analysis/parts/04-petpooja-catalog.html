Offline and architecture (Phase 12 input)

PETPOOJA OFFICIAL POS Local: a Bridge Server runs on a Windows computer at the restaurant and holds a local database with every bill, order and report. Counters read/write it directly; one device is the Master Billing Station and secondary counters, kitchen displays and captain devices connect through it. If the Bridge Server and POS are on the same machine billing runs fully offline; if on separate machines it "continues for up to 5 days offline". Menu/price updates, settings and aggregator orders sync down from Petpooja's cloud when the connection returns; pending online orders "catch up on their own". Scheduled local backups (daily/weekly/monthly) go to a separate drive. The cloud POS variant also works through drops and syncs bills when connectivity returns. Conflict-resolution rules are UNKNOWN.
