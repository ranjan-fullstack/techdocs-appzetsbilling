APPZETBILLING — ODISHA #1 ROADMAP

25.17 The strategic question, answered

What must AppZetBilling build, improve and integrate to become a credible Odisha-first restaurant SaaS able to compete with platforms such as Petpooja?

Facts the answer rests on

  • VERIFIED AppZet already has a broad module base and an Odia UI, but is early in real usage (39 business rows, 77 sales rows) and lacks several features restaurants treat as baseline (GST split, split bills, printers, offline, audit).
  • VERIFIED AppZet has no scheduler/worker, no webhooks, and a broken certificate-renewal path — so reliability, not features, is the first credibility risk.
  • PETPOOJA OFFICIAL Petpooja publicly bundles unlimited users/terminals, aggregator integrations, offline POS and 24/7 support, and sells engagement, KDS, QR, reservations and central kitchen as add-ons; it does not name Odia among its languages.
  • THIRD-PARTY Local competitors exist in Bhubaneswar (e.g. Restrofi with a ₹699/outlet/month page and "local onboarding support"); UPI dominates payments; an NRAI Odisha chapter launched in Dec 2025.

Recommendations (in order)

  1. Be trustworthy first: Priority 0 reliability and security; publish uptime; make backups/restore drills routine.
  2. Be correct for Indian restaurants: GST invoice model, UPI QR, split/multi-payment/merge, audit + voids, printer support, offline Level 1.
  3. Be the easy, local, Odia-first choice: onboarding wizard + menu import + assisted setup; finish Odia; local support and content; simple transparent per-outlet pricing.
  4. Be WhatsApp-first: bills, order/reservation/delivery updates, loyalty and festival campaigns in Odia and English, with consent and metering.
  5. Close the ecosystem gap where demand is proven: start aggregator partner applications now; own delivery for local practice; Tally/GST exports for the CA channel.
  6. Prove it with metrics: define "active restaurant", measure time-to-first-bill, conversion, churn, support times; publish case studies.
