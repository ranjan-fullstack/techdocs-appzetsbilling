Verdict RECOMMENDATION SalePro is a retail/ERP product with a restaurant layer, not a restaurant-first POS. For restaurants, AppZetBilling is already ahead on the things that matter operationally (recipes, KOT, reservations, Odia). Do not adopt SalePro for restaurants.

Where SalePro is genuinely strong RECOMMENDATION Electronics (IMEI + repair + installments), garments (variants), wholesale (multi-price, credit, purchase, accounting), bakeries (batch/expiry + weight barcodes), and general retail. A retail vertical brand on SalePro, alongside a restaurant brand on AppZetBilling, is the only combination that uses both products' strengths — at the cost of two licences, two codebases and the licence question in Part 01.
