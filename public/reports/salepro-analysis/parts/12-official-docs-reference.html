Corrections to earlier parts (docs override my earlier "not found")
  • WhatsApp exists (Ch. 18, 21.25, POS): WhatsApp Cloud API (Meta) settings, approved-template sync, send from Customer List, "Send WhatsApp Message" tick-box in the POS payment modal, per-event In-App/WhatsApp/SMS/Email matrix. Sending is mostly staff-triggered, not automatic. Earlier: "NOT FOUND / NOT AVAILABLE" → now PARTIAL.
  • Indian GST invoice format exists (5.3): General Setting → Invoice Format "Indian GST" auto-calculates CGST/SGST/IGST and shows them separately on the receipt. Earlier: "UNK" → now AVAILABLE (test with real invoices). e-invoice and e-way bill are still not mentioned.
  • Activity Log exists (20.1): who created/updated/deleted sales, purchases and quotations. Earlier "audit log likely absent" → PARTIAL (no login, price-change or super-admin coverage documented).
  • Cash register, coupons, discount plans, gift cards, customer credit limits, deposits, reward points, direct ESC/POS network printing, low-stock notification in POS, expiry and quantity-alert reports are all documented → earlier UNK items now AVAILABLE.
  • Offline POS is documented in detail (see Ch. 5.3 below) — browser-only, single device, data lost if browser storage is cleared. This confirms the earlier inference.
  • Restaurant depth is still thin in the docs: only a few paragraphs in 5.3 and "Tables" 21.5. Floors only work "when the Restaurant module is installed/active"; KDS and modifiers are named but have no chapter. Ask whether the Restaurant module is a separate paid item.
  • Manufacturing and Repair are listed as Addons (Ch. 22) and Project/eCommerce/Tailoring/Social Commerce as "enabled modules". The CodeCanyon page says several are included — ask exactly which are in the $30 licence.
  • Recipes: "Wastage Percent" is reference info only and does not change stock deduction; production is a manual entry that adds finished stock and deducts ingredients. Confirms there is no auto-deduction per restaurant sale.
  • SMS is Twilio or Clickatell only (21.14) — no Indian DLT provider (MSG91, etc.). Extra work for India.
  • Languages: the docs mention Bengali as the example; Odia still not confirmed.

6–9. Quotation, transfer, expense, income

  • Quotation: price offer with status, e-mail PDF, convert to sale; no stock or revenue until converted.
  • Transfer: warehouse-to-warehouse with status, shipping cost, CSV import; must have stock in source.
  • Expense: categories, list with filters, receipt attachment, warehouse-tagged; Income: categories, list; posts a credit to the account (no receipt upload).

10. Accounting

Double-entry. Accounts (with default account for checkout), add account, money transfer (journal), balance sheet, account statement with opening balance and running balance. Sales, purchases and payments post automatically; edits/deletes reverse the earlier effect rather than rewriting history; periodic inventory for COGS/profit (not per-sale COGS); Accounting Health/reconciliation tool for admins. Not documented: trial balance in this chapter (product page mentions it), Tally/GSTR export.

11. HRM

Department, designation, shift, employee (payroll is tied to the employee record, not the user), attendance, holiday, overtime, leave type, leaves, payroll (sale-agent commission flows in), HRM settings (default check-in/out).

12. Manufacturing (add-on)

Recipe (Standard or Combo products; ingredient rows with quantity, unit cost/price, wastage % as reference), Production (select recipe, quantity, cost → finished stock added, ingredients deducted), production details and calculation examples. Manual, not per-sale.

17–19. AI Assistant, WhatsApp, Social Commerce

  • AI Assistant (17): a fixed library of ten questions answered from your own data (sales/purchase/expense summaries, daily snapshot, low stock, top and slow sellers, customer/supplier dues, cash and bank). Not a free-form chatbot; no external AI API or cost; respects warehouse restriction. (Consistent with the CodeCanyon "no external API" claim.)
  • WhatsApp (18): Cloud API credentials (permanent token, phone-number ID, business-account ID); templates are approved in Meta and only listed/deleted in SalePro; send from Customer List; falls back to wa.me link without credentials.
  • Social Commerce (19): dashboard by channel (WhatsApp/Facebook/Instagram/Telegram), publishable product catalog with share links and QR, Meta Commerce CSV feed with secret URL, settings including three staff-triggered WhatsApp text templates (order confirmation, payment request, delivery update).

What this changes for the decision

  • SalePro is broader than I first reported (WhatsApp Cloud API, Indian GST invoice, activity log, cash register, tailoring, social commerce, 34 reports). Retail and wholesale fit improves; the AI, HRM and accounting claims hold up.
  • The restaurant verdict does not improve: KDS, kitchens, modifiers and floors are only referenced, with no set-up documentation, and there is still no automatic ingredient deduction, aggregator integration, split/merge bill or captain app in the docs.
  • India gaps that remain: e-invoice/e-way bill, Indian SMS (DLT) providers, Odia language and printing, Tally/GSTR exports, UPI beyond Razorpay's UPI-only POS flow.
