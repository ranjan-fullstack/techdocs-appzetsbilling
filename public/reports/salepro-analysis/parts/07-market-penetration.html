What the scenarios imply

  • Base Year 1 (200 tenants) already exceeds the moderate break-even (165) on a 3-person team; the conservative case (80) never breaks even in Year 1 ESTIMATE — see Part 06.
  • Base needs ≈ 50 new paying customers per quarter in Year 1 rising to ≈ 230/quarter in Year 3, which is 3–4 productive salespeople plus channel partners ESTIMATE.
  • Penetration of 3% of the Odisha SAM by Year 3 is not "small": with Petpooja-type incumbents plus local billing-machine dealers and free tools, plausible but not guaranteed INFERENCE. Treat 1,600 as a stretch that needs proof from the first 100 customers.
