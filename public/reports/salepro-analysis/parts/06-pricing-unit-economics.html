15. Pricing strategy

What the market anchors to THIRD-PARTY

  • Lowest: Loyverse free core, DotPe free-to-start, Restrofi ≈ ₹699/outlet/month, Vyapar ≈ ₹3,399/year (≈ ₹283/month).
  • Mainstream: Petpooja ≈ ₹10k–20k/outlet/year (≈ ₹830–1,670/month) before GST; GoFrugal from ≈ ₹500/user/month.

What the numbers say (1) At ₹550 ARPU the business cannot afford the human support model it needs at 500 customers (4.3 people affordable vs 9 needed) — cheap plans require self-serve onboarding, in-app help and Odia video training. (2) The licence and hosting costs are trivial next to team cost; the deciding variable is how many customers one support/onboarding person can carry. (3) Growth is capital-constrained: CAC of ₹4–8k × 165 customers ≈ ₹7–13 lakh to reach break-even, plus P0 development ₹7–13 lakh (Part 05) — budget roughly ₹20–30 lakh to reach break-even ESTIMATE. (4) Churn is not yet in the model: at 3% monthly churn (≈ 31%/yr ASSUMPTION), gross adds needed to stay flat at 500 customers are 15/month.
Revenue streams that look realistic RECOMMENDATION Subscriptions (core) · setup/onboarding fee ₹1,500–5,000 for restaurants (helps CAC) · WhatsApp/SMS credits at cost +25–40% · extra outlet/user add-ons · hardware bundles via dealers at thin margin (referral fee, not inventory) · reseller margins. Weak or slow: ecommerce upsell, API monetisation, custom integrations before you have 500+ customers.
