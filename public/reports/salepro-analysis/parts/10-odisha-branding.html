D. Odisha-first go-to-market

  • Vertical first: our recommendation RECOMMENDATION is retail + cafés first (lower operational complexity, faster onboarding, no aggregator dependency), with restaurants following once KOT printing, recipes and offline are solid. If AppZetBilling stays the core, restaurants can lead because it already has that depth.
  • City order: Bhubaneswar and Cuttack (wave 1), then Puri, Berhampur, Sambalpur, Rourkela, then Balasore, Jharsuguda, Angul, Baripada (Part 09). Industrial towns (Jharsuguda, Angul, Rourkela) offer canteens and wholesale trade; connectivity should be validated UNKNOWN.
  • Typical business size: 1 outlet, 2–5 staff, ₹5–50 lakh annual turnover ASSUMPTION; subscription sensitivity high — annual prepay discount and free trial are the conversion levers.
  • Local sales: 2-person field pairs, 10 demos/day, WhatsApp follow-up in Odia; dealer bundles with printer sellers; CA partnerships for GST-heavy retail; referral credit (one free month) for both sides; local business associations (traders' and hotel-restaurant associations) for group demos.
  • Digital: Google search "billing software Bhubaneswar", Instagram/YouTube shorts in Odia, WhatsApp click-to-chat; small budget first.
