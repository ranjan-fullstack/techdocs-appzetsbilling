30. Management summary

  • SalePro + SalePro SaaS is a credible, cheap starting point for a retail-oriented SaaS; the licence cost is negligible (≈ ₹22k for Extended ×2 [[ASM]]) and the technology can serve hundreds of tenants.
  • It is not a restaurant-first product, and you already own a restaurant-strong SaaS.
  • Economics: break-even ≈ 165 paying customers at ₹860 ARPU with a 3-person team [[EST]]; requires about ₹20–30 lakh of runway [[EST]].
  • Biggest unknowns: licence interpretation, tenant isolation quality, offline behaviour, Odia support, and vendor dependence.
  • Recommended next step: a 2–3 week evidence-gathering sprint, not a purchase.
