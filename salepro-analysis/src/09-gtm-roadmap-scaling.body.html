Geographic expansion logic

WaveCities/statesRationale
1Bhubaneswar → Cuttack (twin-city cluster)Highest density of cafés, cloud kitchens, modern retail; existing local competitors show demand [[TP]]; one team covers both
2Puri, Berhampur, Sambalpur, RourkelaTourism restaurants (Puri, seasonal), regional trade hubs, industrial canteens; requires per-city partners
3Balasore, Jharsuguda, Angul, BaripadaSmaller markets; serve by remote onboarding + dealer partners
4West Bengal, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Tamil Nadu, KarnatakaOrder by language reuse and partner availability: Bengali/Telugu/Tamil/Kannada UI; West Bengal shares borders and business links with Odisha [[INF]]

Does the expansion strategy make sense? Yes for waves 1–2, because local trust and Odia support are cheap advantages; do not leave Odisha until you hit a measurable threshold (e.g. 500 paying tenants, monthly churn under 3%, CAC payback under 9 months) [[REC]]. Neighbouring-state expansion should follow partners and language readiness, not map adjacency.
